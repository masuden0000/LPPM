Description

This textbook provides an in-depth exploration of research methodologies specific to the social sciences. Designed primarily for undergraduate and graduate students, it bridges the gap between theoretical frameworks and practical application in field research.

Key topics include structuring a research proposal, ethical considerations in human subjects research, advanced survey design, ethnographic observation, and statistical analysis using contemporary software tools. The author draws on extensive field experience to provide contextual examples that resonate with institutional research standards.
