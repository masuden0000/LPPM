Epidemiological Methods in Public Health

By Dr. Siti Nurbaya, MPH.

Rp 125.000 Rp 150.000

Synopsis

This comprehensive text provides a rigorous foundation in epidemiological methods, tailored specifically for the context of public health challenges in developing nations. Dr. Nurbaya draws upon extensive field research to present theoretical concepts alongside practical, real-world case studies.

The book covers study design, measures of disease frequency, bias, confounding, and an introduction to statistical modeling. It is designed as a core textbook for undergraduate and graduate students in public health, nursing, and medicine, as well as a vital reference for practicing public health professionals aiming to strengthen their analytical skills.

person About the Author
